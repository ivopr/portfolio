## Platform

web

## Users
Two audiences with equal weight:
- People hiring full-stack / React developers, deciding whether to reach out.
- Research collaborators (labs, bioinformatics and health researchers) deciding whether Ivo can build their scientific software.

## Product Purpose
Personal portfolio of Ivo Henrique Provensi Vieira, bilingual (English + Portuguese). Success: a visitor leaves knowing Ivo builds the web tools scientists actually use, and knows how to reach Ivo by email, GitHub, LinkedIn, or Lattes.

## Positioning
A full-stack developer who ships research software from inside a research institution (Fiocruz Rondônia, LABIOQUIM). The proof is a peer-reviewed paper about Ivo's own app (Visual Dynamics, BMC Bioinformatics 2023). Few developer portfolios can claim that.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, Tailwind 4, shadcn (base-ui), static. Locales are `/en` and `/pt`.
- Content comes only from GitHub, the Lattes CV (Feb 2024 PDF and the May 2026 update), the LinkedIn profile export (Oct 2026), and the previous ivopr.com.br. Nothing else may be invented.
- Personal identifiers in the Lattes PDF (CPF, RG, parents, birth date) never appear on the site.

## Brand Commitments
- Refined, technical, precise. No playful or handwritten type, no lab-prop metaphors (user rejected the gel world, 2026-10-30).
- Keep the personal voice: video games, a cat, manga. Footer line: "The horrors persist, but so do I."
